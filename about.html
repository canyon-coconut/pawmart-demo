Our story

PawMart began in 2016 as one shelf of pet food in the Okafor family's corner shop. Word travelled the way it does in Springfield — neighbour to neighbour — and the shelf became an aisle, then the whole shop.

Everything on our shelves is food, toys and care we have used with our own two dogs and a very particular cat. If it does not pass them, it does not get stocked. That is the whole secret, and it has worked for ten years.

Today we deliver across the Springfield area, but the rule has not changed: honest products, fair prices, and advice that costs nothing.

Questions we hear a lot

How fast is delivery?

Order before 4pm and it leaves the shop the same day, anywhere in the Springfield area. Most orders arrive the same evening or the next morning. Delivery is a flat $4.99, and free on orders over $40.

What if my pet will not eat the food?

Bring it back within 30 days — opened or not — for a full refund or a swap. Fussy eaters are a fact of life; you should not be the one paying for them.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, or card at checkout. Whichever suits you — there is no extra charge for either.

Can I collect from the shop instead?

Of course. Order as usual, choose to collect, and we will have it bagged at the counter at 214 Oak Street. We are open until 7pm on weekdays.

Do you stock prescription or specialist food?

We keep the common specialist lines in store. If your vet has named a specific one, call us on (555) 214-0194 and we will order it in — usually within two days.
